# StudyOS PRD

StudyOS is a focused Grade 10 learning operating system for Kirubel. It turns uploaded textbooks into searchable concepts, adaptive questions, fast answer evaluation, targeted remediation, spaced review, and optional voice tutoring.

## MVP
- Supabase-authenticated dashboard with subject mastery and review queue.
- Textbook upload/ingestion into chunks, concepts, and embeddings.
- Active recall study loop graded by Jev, with Gemini remediation on weak answers.
- Gemini Live voice study room scaffold.

## Personas
- Kirubel: Grade 10 learner studying Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics.
- Future educator/guardian: read-only progress observer.
